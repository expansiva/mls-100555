Site Monitoring Plugin

This plugin provides real-time insights into your website's performance, helping you monitor crucial metrics like error rates and response times. With easy-to-read graphs and detailed information, you can quickly identify issues and optimize your web application's reliability and speed.

Key Features

  • Error Monitoring: Track common errors such as 400 and 404, helping you identify issues affecting the user experience.
  • Response Time Tracking: View average response times over specific time intervals, ensuring your site maintains optimal performance.
  • Real-Time Data: Get up-to-the-minute data on the health of your site, allowing for proactive maintenance and fast response to issues.
  • Resource Usage: Monitor AWS resource usage (CPU, memory, network) to understand how your infrastructure is being utilized.
  • Customizable Graphs: Visualize data through customizable charts that provide a clear view of your website's health and performance.
  • Uptime Monitoring: Ensure your site remains available with uptime tracking and alerting for outages.

Benefits of Using This Plugin

  • Improved Performance: By monitoring response times and errors, you can quickly identify and resolve performance bottlenecks.
  • Proactive Issue Resolution: Real-time alerts and data help you address potential problems before they impact users.
  • Better User Experience: Reduced downtime and faster response times translate to a smoother, more reliable experience for your users.
  • Scalability Insights: Track resource usage to help plan for scaling your infrastructure efficiently, avoiding overuse or underutilization of resources.
  • Cost Savings: By monitoring AWS resources, you can optimize costs and avoid unnecessary spending on unused capacity.

Start using this plugin today to gain better visibility into your website's performance and ensure it runs smoothly at all times.
